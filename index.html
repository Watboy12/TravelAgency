    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I open an account with ExploreWorld?

    Click 'Create Account' in the navigation. Fill in your full name, username, email, and a secure password (at least 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, and numbers). After registering, you'll be prompted to verify your identity via our support team before booking any trips.

    What payment methods are accepted?

    We accept bank transfers and Bitcoin (BTC) deposits. Bank transfers require a verified name matching your account, while BTC deposits are credited within 10–60 minutes after blockchain confirmation.

    How long does it take to process a deposit?

    Bank deposits are processed within 1–3 business days depending on your bank. Crypto deposits (BTC) are credited within 10–60 minutes once the transaction is confirmed on the blockchain.

    Can I cancel or change my trip?

    Yes, cancellations or changes are possible up to 30 days before departure, subject to our terms. Contact our support team to discuss your options. Refer to our Terms & Conditions for the full refund schedule.

    What's included in the deluxe packages?

    Deluxe packages vary by destination but typically include flights, premium accommodations, guided tours, and exclusive experiences. Check the 'Learn More' section for each destination for full details.
